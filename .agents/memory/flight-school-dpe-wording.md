---
name: Flight school DPE wording
description: Required wording for checkride scheduling and examiner availability claims.
---

Checkride copy may say the school coordinates examiner planning early and targets a checkride within 1–2 weeks after training completion/readiness when independent DPE schedules, weather, and eligibility permit. It must state that timing is not guaranteed and that the school does not control DPE calendars or outcomes.

**Why:** Examiner availability is external to the school; unconditional timing promises and competitor wait-time comparisons create factual, regulatory, and consistency risks.

**How to apply:** Use this wording on certificate, rating, instructor, cost, and location pages whenever discussing checkride scheduling. Do not reintroduce “ensure,” guaranteed timing, or fixed competitor wait claims.
